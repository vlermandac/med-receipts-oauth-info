Personal-use application

Privacy policy

This policy describes the owner's private installation of Med Receipts rclone.

Data access and use

The application uses Google's OAuth authorization to let rclone read, create, update, move, and delete Drive files. The Google permission covers Drive files; the configured mount uses a dedicated folder containing receipts, supporting documents, processing state, requirements, and results.

Files are used to organize documents and carry out the account owner's instructed receipt workflow. Source documents are preserved and processed results are saved in the owner's Drive folder.

Storage and sharing

Files remain in the owner's Google Drive and may be cached or temporarily processed on the owner's computer. OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored privately on that computer, outside the Drive workflow folder and this website.

When the owner instructs an AI agent to process documents, relevant content may be sent to the AI provider used for that session. Claim documents may also be sent to insurance or health-plan services designated by the owner. Those providers apply their own privacy and retention policies.

This installation does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control and retention

The owner controls the Drive files and local installation. Files are retained until the owner removes them. Access can be revoked in the Google Account connections settings. Revoking access stops future authorized API access but does not remove files already stored in Drive or local caches.

This website

This static information website has no login, upload form, analytics, or application tracking scripts. It does not receive receipts, Google account credentials, or OAuth tokens. The hosting provider may process ordinary web-request information under its own privacy policy.

Questions and updates

This is a single-owner application. The owner maintains its configuration and this policy. Any future change to data use should be reflected here before it is applied.
